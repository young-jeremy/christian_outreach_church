Two-Factor Authentication

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your account by requiring a second verification step when signing in. This helps protect your account and the church community from unauthorized access.

If you want to enable this setting, click on the button below to set up Two-Factor Authentication.

Set Up 2FA

Why use Two-Factor Authentication?

Enabling 2FA helps protect sensitive church information and ensures that only you can access your account, even if someone else knows your password.
