Christian Mentorship Guidelines

Program Overview

Our Christian mentorship program is designed to foster spiritual growth and development through guided relationships with experienced mentors. We believe in the power of discipleship and sharing wisdom across generations.

Mentor Expectations

  • Spiritual Leadership

    Demonstrate mature faith and consistent spiritual practices

  • Commitment

    Regular availability for sessions and prayer support

  • Biblical Foundation

    Ground teaching and guidance in Scripture

Mentee Expectations

  • Active Participation

    Regular attendance and engagement in sessions

  • Personal Growth

    Commitment to spiritual development and applying teachings

  • Respectful Communication

    Open and honest dialogue while maintaining boundaries

Session Structure

Component Duration Description
Opening Prayer 5 mins Setting spiritual focus for the session
Scripture Reading 10 mins Reading and initial reflection on selected passages
Main Teaching 30-40 mins Core lesson and spiritual instruction
Discussion 20-25 mins Interactive dialogue and application
Closing Prayer 5-10 mins Reflection and commitment to action

Scripture Foundation

"And the things you have heard me say in the presence of many witnesses entrust to reliable people who will also be qualified to teach others."

2 Timothy 2:2 (NIV)

"Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm."

Proverbs 13:20 (NIV)
