Session Guidelines

Tips for Creating Sessions
  • Choose a clear, focused topic
  • Select relevant Scripture passages
  • Plan for interactive discussion
  • Consider your target audience
  • Allow time for prayer and reflection
Time Management
  • Sessions should be 60-90 minutes
  • Include time for:
    • Opening prayer (5 mins)
    • Scripture reading (10 mins)
    • Main teaching (30-40 mins)
    • Discussion (20-25 mins)
    • Closing prayer (5-10 mins)
